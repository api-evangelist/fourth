# Fourth

> Fourth is an AI-powered workforce management and inventory platform built for restaurants and hospitality, uniting recruiting, HR, payroll, labor scheduling, purchasing and inventory (including the HotSchedules and Adaco products) across 120,000+ locations globally. Fourth exposes a developer portal of REST APIs for POS, inventory/recipe, workforce and identity integration. Production and test host locations are provisioned per mutual customer and are not publicly disclosed.

## APIs
- [POS Transaction API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/pos-gateway/transaction-api-reference): Point-of-sale transaction integration.
- [POS Gateway API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/pos-gateway-old/reference): Legacy POS gateway.
- [RME Export API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/recipe-menu-engineering-export-api/reference): Recipe & Menu Engineering export.
- [RME Import API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/recipe-menu-engineering-import-api/reference): Recipe & Menu Engineering import.
- [Menu Cycles API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/menu-cycles-api/reference): Menu cycle management.
- [Fourth Account SCIM API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/fourth-account-scim-api/reference): SCIM 2.0 user/identity provisioning.
- [Notifications API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/notifications-api/reference): Platform notifications (OAuth scope NotificationService).
- [Tasks API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/tasks-api/reference): Task management.
- [UK Employee API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/uk-employee-api/reference): UK workforce/employee data.
- [Labor Productivity API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/labor-productivity-api/reference): Labor productivity metrics.
- [Schedules API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/schedules-api/reference): Employee scheduling.
- [Adaco Web API](https://developer.fourth.com/en-gb/docs/adaco-product-catalog-api/reference): Adaco hotel inventory (product catalogue, vendor, budget, requisitions, inventory).
